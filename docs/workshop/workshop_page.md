[h1]Story Mode[/h1]
Halcyon Minerals has landed you on a hostile planet with a dome, a drill and a quota. Dig down, sell what you find, buy better gear, and keep the dome standing against the waves.

Something deep below is glad you came.

[*]Let it reach the surface under your dome and it builds up inside. Vent the dome from the shop.
